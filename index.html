What is a First-Then board?

A First-Then board shows two pictures side by side: First a task, Then something the child wants. “First shoes, then park.”

It makes the day predictable and gives a clear reason to start a hard task. Parents, teachers and therapists use it for autism, ADHD, speech therapy and with toddlers. It is often the first step before a full visual schedule.

Why First-Then works

The idea behind the board is called the Premack principle: first the less-wanted task, then the wanted one. The thing a child likes to do becomes the reason to start the thing that is harder.

When to move on: when your child follows First-Then (and First-Then-Next) calmly most of the time, try a full visual schedule that shows more steps of the day.

How to fade the board out: do it little by little. Show the board only for the hard moments, later just say the words “first…, then…”, and stop using it when your child no longer needs it.

How to make a First-Then board

  1. Choose the task

    Tap ✏️ on the First card. Pick an icon, take a photo, or type a word for one small task.

  2. Choose the reward

    Tap ✏️ on the Then card. Put something there that your child really wants.

  3. Add more pairs

    Need more than one? Tap ＋ under the cards to add the next pair.

  4. Show it or print it

    Use the board on the screen, or open the ⋯ menu → Print for a paper board.

How to use this board

  1. Pick the pictures

    Tap ✏️ on a card (or ＋ on an empty card). Choose an icon, a photo, or type a word.

  2. Show one pair

    Say it simply: “First homework, then tablet.” Point to each card.

  3. Tap when done

    When the task is done, tap the First card. The circle gets a ✓ and Then lights up.

  4. Keep your promise

    Give the reward right away. Add more pairs with ＋ under the cards, and tap Next to move on.

Tip: tap ⛶ for full screen, so the child sees only the board. Exit by holding ✕ for one second. On iPhone, open the board from the Home Screen icon for true full screen.

Free printable First-Then board

You can print this board for free. Open the ⋯ menu → Print and choose “This pair” or “All pairs”. Each pair prints on its own page, in landscape, with your icons and photos included.

Want an empty one? Choose “Blank template”. It prints empty First and Then cards (and a Next card in 3-card mode), each with space for a picture and a line for the word. Draw, glue a photo or write on it by hand.

A paper first then chart is handy on the fridge, in a school bag, or anywhere a screen is not a good idea.

Tips

🧩 Autism

  • Use real photos of real things: your sink, their tablet.
  • Keep words short and the same every time.
  • Start with an easy task and a strong reward, so the board means “good things happen”.

⚡ ADHD

  • Make “First” small and concrete: “3 math problems”, not “homework”.
  • Add more pairs to break a big job into short pairs.
  • Add a visual timer next to the board if waiting is hard.

🧸 Toddlers

  • Two cards only. Big pictures, one word each.
  • Rewards can be tiny: bubbles, a song, a hug.
  • Let the child tap the card. The circle gets a ✓ — it feels great to be the one who finishes.

Using your own photos

Many children understand a photo of the real thing better than an icon. Tap ✏️ → 📷 Photo, take a picture or choose one, and crop it to a square. The photo is made smaller on your device and saved in My photos, ready to use on any card.

Your photos never leave your device. Nothing is uploaded — not to us and not to anyone else.

FAQ

Is it free?

Yes. The board is free in your browser. No account, no ads.

Where are my photos stored?

Only on this device, inside your browser's storage (IndexedDB). They are never uploaded to a server and never sent to analytics. If you use another phone or browser, open the ⋯ menu → Backup → Export, and Import it there.

Can my boards get lost?

They can, because they live only in this browser. Clearing browser data deletes them, and Safari may delete site data after some days without a visit. To stay safe: add the board to your Home Screen, and export a backup now and then.

Can I print it?

Yes. Open the ⋯ menu → Print, and choose “this pair” or “all pairs”. You get a clean paper board with your icons and photos. “Blank template” prints empty cards that you fill in by hand.

What is First-Then-Next?

It adds a third card (⋯ menu → 3 cards). Use it when the child is ready to see one more step, for example “First bath, then story, next bed”.

Can I have more than one board?

Yes. The board starts with five boards: My board, Morning, Bedtime, School and Meals. Tap the board name above the cards to switch, or add your own. Each board keeps its own pairs. You can also start a new board from a template: Morning, Bedtime, School or Meals.

Does it work on iPad and iPhone?

Yes, in Safari. On iPad, ⛶ gives real full screen. On iPhone, Safari keeps its bars; for full screen, add the board to the Home Screen and open it from there.

Does it work offline?

Yes, after the first visit. The board is saved in your browser, so it opens without internet. Your boards and photos are already on your device.

Is there an app?

Not yet. Tap “Want this as an iPhone/iPad app?” to tell us you want one. For full daily schedules try Visual Schedule, and for rewards try Token Board.

Do you track my child?

No cookies, no ads, no accounts. We count anonymous events, like how often Print is used, to improve the app. Card words, board names and photos never leave your device.
